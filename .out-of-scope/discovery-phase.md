# A discovery phase (framing a need before it is an issue) in the harness

- **Asked in:** [#144](https://github.com/francoisgn/belayline-agentic-harness/issues/144) (LinkedIn comment on the
  launch post, 2026-11-18, comparing Belayline with Spec Kit and Superpowers)
- **Decision:** declined on 2026-11-18
- **Why:** it fails the toolbox test of [SCOPE.md](../SCOPE.md): framing a need is the team's product practice, and the
  harness stays generic by starting at a ready issue (METHODOLOGY §2 bis). No consumer project has reported work lost
  for lack of it. Tools that do it (Superpowers' brainstorming, Spec Kit's assessment extension, a PO workshop) plug
  in before `/intent` ([ADOPTION](../docs/ADOPTION.md#plugging-a-step-before-or-after-the-loop)).
- **What would change the answer:** consumer projects where `/intent` repeatedly ends in `status: needs-decision`
  because the need was never framed, even with a discovery step of their own.
